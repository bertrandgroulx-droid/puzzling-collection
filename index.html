Puzzling

Eight quick word games for your phone. Each one has a new puzzle every day, the previous 100 days to catch up on, and a practice mode with random puzzles. No sign-up, nothing to install.

  • Common Thread

    Three words, and one missing word that pairs with every one of them.

    Word association · Lateral thinking

  • Whittle

    Take one letter away at a time to match three clues before the clock runs out.

    Quick recall · Working under a clock

  • Tumble

    Rearrange the letters to fit the clue, then find a bonus word in the same letters.

    Pattern spotting · Quick recall

  • Stowaway

    Two clues, two words: the second is the first with one letter smuggled in.

    Word building · Deduction

  • Soundalike

    Two clues, two words that sound the same but are spelled differently.

    Sounding words out · Spelling

  • Switcheroo

    Change one letter at a time to climb a three-step word ladder.

    Step-by-step thinking · Working under a clock

  • Three of a Kind

    Three words have something in common. Spot what it is.

    Lateral thinking · General knowledge

  • Splice

    Two clues, two words, blended into one where they overlap.

    Word blending · An ear for sound

Scores are kept on this device only. Every game has a How to play panel behind the ? button, with a timer switch and colour-blind colours in its settings.
